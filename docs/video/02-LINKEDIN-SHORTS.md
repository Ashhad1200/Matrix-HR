# MatrixHR — 20 LinkedIn Short Scripts
*Vertical shorts, ~25–45 seconds each. Each: **Hook** (first 2s), **Script** (spoken, ~90–120 words), **On-screen** (text/visual beats), **CTA**, and a ready **Caption** for the post. Keep one idea per video. Hooks are the whole game — the first line stops the scroll.*

> Honesty rules for every short: no invented stats, no fake logos. Where tax or rates appear, add the caption *"Confirm current rates with your advisor."* For the monitoring short, always lead with transparency.

## 1 — "HR runs in five places. Payroll pays for it."
**Hook:** "Your payroll is wrong before you even open it."
**Script:** Most companies run HR in five places — a spreadsheet for people, a machine for attendance, WhatsApp for leave, a notebook for loans, and an accountant re-typing all of it at month-end. Every hand-off is a place to lose money. A missed unpaid day. A loan instalment nobody deducted. MatrixHR puts it all on one employee record, so payroll is built from live data — not re-typed from memory. Everything HR does ends up on a payslip. So we made the payslip the source of truth.
**On-screen:** Five icons tangling → one clean line into a payslip.
**CTA:** "One record. One payslip. Link in comments."
**Caption:** Running HR across a spreadsheet, a biometric machine, WhatsApp and a notebook? Every hand-off is where payroll breaks. Here's the fix. #HR #Payroll #Pakistan #HRTech

## 2 — "Payroll should take two people."
**Hook:** "The person who prepares payroll should never approve it."
**Script:** The payroll mistakes that cost the most are the ones nobody else looked at. So in MatrixHR, one person prepares the run — and a different person has to approve it. The system won't let you sign off on your own work. The preparer builds it; the checker catches it. It's the oldest control in accounting, finally built into payroll software. Approve it, and out comes a payslip for everyone, a bank file to pay them all at once, and a journal for your accountant.
**On-screen:** "Prepare" by User A → "Approve" greyed out for A, live for B.
**CTA:** "Maker-checker payroll. See it live."
**Caption:** Would you let one person write AND approve the cheque? Then why does payroll work that way? Maker-checker, built in. #Payroll #FinanceControls #HRTech

## 3 — "Approve leave from WhatsApp."

**On-screen:** WhatsApp thread → green tick → balance updates on a laptop behind.
**CTA:** "Approvals where your team already is."
**Caption:** Managers don't live in dashboards. They live in WhatsApp. So approvals live there too — with a full audit trail. #WhatsApp #HR #Leave #Pakistan

- **Reseller series:** #13 plus custom cuts of #1, #6, #12 target partners; everything else targets HR leaders and founders.
