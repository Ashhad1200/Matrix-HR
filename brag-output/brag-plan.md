# Brag Plan: MatrixHR

## What is this app?
A multi-tenant HR and payroll platform built for South Asia and the Middle East, with Pakistani payroll (tax slabs, EOBI, PF) that global HR tools never localized. 48 screens and 76 data models, in three role-based portals.

## The angle
Every generic HR tool skips the acronyms a Pakistani payroll admin deals with every month. The video opens on those acronyms as a small wall of dread, then pays them off as tidy checkboxes. The joke is the deadpan understatement: the hardest part of regional payroll is now "just a checkbox." Everything shown is real product behaviour: the payroll run's four-state approval chain and the approvals inbox.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark green frame. Three terms type out one line at a time: **FBR tax.** / **EOBI.** / **PF.** Then, quietly: *Global HR software never learned them.* The hook is the recognition. Anyone who runs payroll here reads it and nods.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The wordmark reveal: the "M" mark and **MatrixHR**, with the product's own tagline.
- **The payroll run, working.** A payroll card whose status badge walks DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → LOCKED as a cursor clicks Submit for Review, Approve, then Lock & Generate Payslips. The help text under the badge changes with each state (real strings from the product).
- **The approvals inbox.** Three pending requests arrive one by one; the cursor approves the first and the counter ticks down.

## Outro / punchline
The three hook terms return as chips and check themselves off one by one. Then: **Now they're checkboxes.** Cut to the M mark and MatrixHR wordmark, held, with the music fading under it.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Payroll run:** entry (a DRAFT run) → key action (submit, approve, lock) → result (LOCKED. Payslips are generated).
2. **Approvals:** entry (inbox with pending items) → key action (Approve) → result (Pending Items count drops).

- Creative direction: dry regional-SaaS launch. The paperwork nobody else localized.
- Interpretation: Clean, warm and confident, with the humor coming from understatement. Fast motion, adequate holds, no shouting. Copy is short and plain; the product UI carries the story.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (about 120 BPM, upbeat and corporate-warm)
- Music treatment: start at 0:00 at a low bed level, ducked lightly under the hook; lift slightly at the reveal; fade out over the last ~1.5s under the final logo hold.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`.../cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`), tempo 120.19 BPM, beat grid starts at 3.02s.
  - Scene cuts snap to grid beats: 4.53s (reveal), 9.02s (payroll), 14.02s (approvals), 17.02s (outro).
  - Strong-cue lock: **17.02s**, the outro "chips" slam (strong_beat, 1.00). Optional second lock: 16.02s, the approve click.
  - Beat-grid windows: payroll clicks at 10.02, 11.52 and 12.52; approval cards at 14.52, 15.02 and 15.52.
  - Readable-text note: the approval cards carry text, so they arrive quickly and then the full set holds about 1s before the click. Do not add more text reveals on the beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Use music RMS/bass to make the blurred emerald glow behind the hook and outro breathe, and give the recreated UI card slightly more presence on strong hits. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed hook (keypress ticks), cursor clicks (soft UI click), card arrivals (subtle), checkmark chips (small tick), final logo (one low, dry hit).
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, risers, or comedic stings. Nothing that competes with reading.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.5s (0.0-4.5)
Dark green gradient with soft blurred emerald orbs. Left-aligned display type. Three lines type out one at a time: "FBR tax." then "EOBI." then "PF." (about 0.3s / 0.9s / 1.5s). After they settle, a smaller line fades in beneath: "Global HR software never learned them." Line 2 needs about 2.0s settled on screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Three terms typed one by one; each finishes before the next starts. Hold the full set on screen; do not remove earlier lines.
Audio intent: quiet tension; the music bed is barely there.
Audio-coupled idea: keypress ticks on each typed character.
Music: warm bed, low, before the beat grid starts at 3.02s.
Transition mood: hard cut on beat 4.53s → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 4.5s (4.5-9.0)
The rounded "M" tile and "MatrixHR" wordmark scale in on the beat, as on the login page. Below: "The connected HR platform / built for South Asia and the Middle East." (the product's own tagline, two lines, needs about 2.9s settled).
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: relief, the bed lifts slightly.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: warm, lifts.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Payroll, working — 5.0s (9.0-14.0)
The recreated Payroll screen (light theme card on the dark frame): "Payroll 2026-08" in mono, a status badge, help text, and action buttons. Lower-third caption: "Payroll that needs a second pair of eyes." (about 2.1s settled). A cursor clicks:
1. **Submit for Review** at 10.02s. Badge → REVIEW. Help text: "Awaiting approval from someone other than the preparer."
2. **Approve** at 11.52s. Badge → APPROVED. Help text: "Approved — lock to generate and archive payslips."
3. **Lock & Generate Payslips** at 12.52s. Badge → LOCKED. Help text: "Locked. Payslips are generated."
Sequential/interaction: yes. Three simulated clicks, each changing the badge state and the buttons. The REVIEW state holds about 1.5s and LOCKED holds about 1.4s; only the REVIEW help text and the caption are meant to be read.
Audio intent: satisfying, precise.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft UI click on each cursor press, matched to the frame the badge flips.
Music: steady groove.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Approvals — 3.0s (14.0-17.0)
The recreated Approvals screen: title "Approvals", subtitle "Review and action pending requests", "Pending Items (3)". Three request cards arrive one by one on beats 14.52 / 15.02 / 15.52 (for example "Annual Leave · Sep 22 – Sep 24 / Sara Ahmed", plus two more in the same shape). The cursor clicks **Approve** on the first at 16.52s; the counter ticks to (2). Caption: "Every request. One inbox."
Sequential/interaction: yes. Three cards arrive quickly, then the set holds about 1s before the click. Keep each card to two short lines.
Audio intent: momentum building into the outro.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle card-arrival sounds on each card, one click on Approve.
Music: builds toward the strong cue at 17.02s.
Transition mood: hard cut on strong cue 17.02s → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Punchline + logo — 4.0s (17.0-21.0)
On the strong cue (17.02s): three chips slam in, "FBR tax", "EOBI", "PF", then check themselves off one by one (about 0.4s apart, small non-text-reading ticks). Line: "Now they're checkboxes." (3 words, needs about 1.2s settled). Then the chips and line clear and the "M" tile with "MatrixHR" holds for the last ~1.5s while the music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Chips check off one at a time. This is an accent (ticks), not new text.
Audio intent: payoff, then a clean landing.
Audio-coupled idea: a small tick sound per checkmark, and one low, dry hit on the final logo.
Music: peak on the cue, then fade under the logo.
Transition mood: end on hold, then fade out.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, professional
